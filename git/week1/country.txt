🇬🇭 Ghana: Nature & Cultural HeritageKakum National ParkWhat it is: A lush rainforest famous for its exhilarating canopy walkway suspended 30 meters above the ground.The experience: Walking across narrow bridges high in the treetops while looking for rare birds and monkeys.Mole National ParkWhat it is: Ghana's largest wildlife refuge located in the northern region.The experience: Going on a walking safari to view elephants, leopards, and over 300 bird species up close.Elmina CastleWhat it is: A UNESCO World Heritage site and the oldest European building in Sub-Saharan Africa.The experience: A deeply moving, historic tour exploring the sobering legacy of the transatlantic slave trade.
🇩🇰 Denmark: Fairytale Castles & CoastsRosenborg Slot (Copenhagen)What it is: A stunning Renaissance palace built in the 1600s located right in the city center.The experience: Stepping inside to see the Danish Crown Jewels and strolling through the beautiful King's Garden.Frederiksborg Slot (Hillerød)What it is: Often called "Denmark's Versailles," this masterpiece sits majestically on three small islands in a lake.The experience: Exploring Scandinavia's largest Renaissance residence and its pristine Baroque gardens.Møns KlintWhat it is: A dramatic stretch of 6-kilometer-long bright white chalk cliffs dropping straight into the Baltic Sea.The experience: Hiking the cliffside paths for panoramic sea views or searching for ancient fossils on the beach below.
