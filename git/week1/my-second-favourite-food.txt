You can make a traditional West African palm-nut soup, known as Sauce Graine (palm-nut sauce), in about an hour and a half using canned palm-nut pulp for convenience.

Ingredients
800 g palm-nut pulp or concentrate
600 g beef, smoked fish, or a mix of both
2 onions3 cloves garlic
2 fresh tomatoes or tomato paste
1 fresh chili pepper (to taste)
1 stock cube, salt, and pepper

instructions
Prep the meat: Cut the beef into chunks and season with salt, pepper, and a bit of onion. Cook or parboil the meat until tender.
Blend the aromatics: Blend or finely chop the onions, garlic, tomatoes, and chili pepper.
Combine: In a large pot, add the palm-nut pulp, the cooked meat, and the blended tomato-onion mixture.
Simmer: Add about 2 to 3 cups of water or leftover meat broth. Stir well and bring to a gentle boil.Cook: Reduce the heat and let it simmer uncovered for 45 to 60 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens and the oil rises slightly to the top.
Serve: Adjust seasoning with the stock cube and salt. 
Serve hot with rice, fonio, or fufu
