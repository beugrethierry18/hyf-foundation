Ivorian Attiéké Garba is a popular street food dish made with fermented cassava couscous (attiéké) and crispy fried tuna.

IngredientsTuna: 
2 large tuna steaks or pieces, salted
Attiéké: 1 package of cassava couscous (attiéké)
Flour: ½ cup all-purpose flour (for coating the fish)
Oil: Vegetable oil for frying
Vegetables: 2 fresh tomatoes, 1 onion, 1 fresh chili pepper (or green pepper), and ½ cucumber, diced
Seasoning: Salt, pepper, and a bouillon cube or flavor enhancer (like Maggi)

Instructions
Prep the fish: Cut the tuna into chunks or steaks. Season with salt and pepper, then coat thoroughly in flour.
Fry the tuna: Heat about half an inch of oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Fry the fish until golden brown and cooked through (about 2–4 minutes per side). Remove the fish and set aside, keeping some of the warm frying oil.
Prepare the attiéké: Steam or moisten the attiéké according to package instructions (or warm it up) and toss with a little salt and a tablespoon or two of the warm frying oil.
Make the vegetable garnish: Chop the tomatoes, onions, cucumbers, and chili peppers into small cubes. Mix them in a bowl with a pinch of salt and a splash of the leftover warm frying oil or vinaigrette.
Serve: Place a generous portion of attiéké on a large plate, top with the crispy fried tuna, and pile the fresh vegetable mixture alongside or on top. 
Enjoy immediately
